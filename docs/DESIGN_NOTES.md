# TME Modern Design Notes

Future versions of the page should keep the current blue-white-black visual direction.

## Palette

- Page background: white.
- Primary text: near-black (`#05070d`).
- Secondary text: cool gray (`#566173`).
- Accent/action blue: muted institutional blue (`#2467a6`).
- Dark blue hover/accent: `#153e67`.
- Soft blue panels: muted pale blue (`#eef3f7`, `#e8eef5`, `#f2f5f8`).
- Borders: avoid visible outlines on content boxes; rely on quiet fills and spacing.

Avoid returning to the previous warm cream/teal/coral palette.

## Typography

- Body font: `Nunito Sans`.
- Display/headline font: `Nunito Sans`, using moderate weights rather than ultra-thin display text.
- Keep the look crisp, academic, and restrained, closer to the CBER blue-white-black contrast feel than to a warm editorial site.

## Identity Treatment

- Do not use a separate TME monogram/logo in the top-left corner.
- The nav should read plainly: `The Microstructure Exchange`.
- If the hero headline emphasizes the initials T/M/E, keep it subtle: no underline and no alternate color.
